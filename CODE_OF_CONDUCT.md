# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Presentation Forge should be a welcoming place for people of every background
and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable
technical feedback and make room for disagreement without making it personal.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- using inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints and levels of experience;
- focusing criticism on code, behavior and evidence;
- accepting responsibility, apologising and correcting mistakes;
- protecting private information shared while diagnosing a problem.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised
attention, insults, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or
any conduct that would reasonably make another contributor feel unsafe.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that violate this standard. Temporary or permanent participation restrictions
may be applied when necessary, proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
[@Deepnar GitHub profile](https://github.com/Deepnar). Do not publish sensitive
details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept as
confidential as practical.

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone publicly represents
the project.
